    Our kitchen

    A taste of Jordan on Cecil B. Moore

    Our falafel is mixed every morning with spices brought over from Jordan. Our mansaf is lamb simmered in jameed, the tangy dried-yogurt sauce that every Jordanian grandmother swears by. The bread is warm, the tea comes with cinnamon and walnuts, and nobody leaves hungry.
